Top-Row KPI Cards

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) [€17.59]: The structural spending threshold required to win a single unique customer.
Return on Ad Spend (ROA) [€4.75]: Financial efficiency metric proving that every €1 allocated to ads returns €4.75 in top-line revenue.
Click-Through Rate (CTR) [0.40%]: Top-of-funnel indicator analyzing consumer engagement with creative assets.
Conversion Rate (CR) [1.71%]: Bottom-of-funnel bottom line measuring clicks that turn into completed transactions.

Matrix Analysis

Performance Matrix (Table): Displays chronological growth across budget boundaries, order volumes, and net new customer generation.
Spend and Target Budget (Gauge Chart): highlighting actual spend ($1.03M) exceeds the foundational target baseline ($386.16K).
Revenue vs. Marketing Spend (Area Chart): mapping visual gap between incoming attributed revenue and outbound marketing costs.
Clicks vs. Impression (Combo Chart): mapping raw ad views (Impressions as lines) alongside direct consumer traffic actions (Clicks as vertical bars) across historical quarterly timelines.

Top-Left KPI Cards

Total Sales [€104.80M]: Top-line metric summarizing gross item sales value before logistics add-ons.
Total Revenue [€108.53M]: Absolute gross intake combining product sales and shipping fees.
CLV [€330.06]: Customer Lifetime Value, calculating long-term gross worth per buyer.
Shipping Fees [€3.74M]: Cumulative logistics expenses charged directly to consumers.
Gross_Profit_Margin [45.40%]: Financial health indicator tracking overall margin retention.
Avg_Order_Value [€69.37]: The baseline mean financial footprint left by an individual basket checkout.

Matrix Analysis

Count of Category by Review Score & Brand Tier (Stacked Bar Chart): Compares total feedback volumes grouped by star ratings (1–5) and split by item class.
Sum of Payment Value by Payment Type (Donut Chart): Highlights transaction preferences.
Total Sales and Gross Profit (Combo Chart): It contrasts raw transaction volume (green vertical columns) directly against baseline retained profits (overlaid green trend line).

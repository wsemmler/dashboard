Marketing Campaign Analyst Dashboard

Note: This dashboard is built entirely using mock dummy data. The resulting generic numbers are for illustrative purposes only and do not represent any specific company case.

Card

Conversion Rate: Explain how much person who clicked Ad and complete a web activity.
Click Through Rate: Explain how much person who clicked Ad after seeing it.
CPA: Explain how much advertisers pay for each conversion.
CPC: Explain how much advertisers pay for each click.

Analysis

Demographic Performance: Breaks down target audience metrics by Age group distributions.
Web Traffic: Compares Website Visits and Social Shares against Time On Site.
AdSpend vs Profit: A timeline tracking relation between advertising costs and actual profit generated.
